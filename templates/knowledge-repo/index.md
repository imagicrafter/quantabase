# Home

The front page of this knowledge repo. Link the pages that matter most here.

## Start here

- Concepts: how things work, in `concepts/`
- Decisions: what was chosen and why, in `decisions/`
- Projects: ongoing work, in `projects/`
- People: who is who, in `people/`
- Meetings: notes, in `meetings/`

## How pages are tagged

Each page names its topic in the block at its top (`domains: [topic-id]`).
The topics are listed in `.kb-engine/engine.yaml`. See `README.md`.
